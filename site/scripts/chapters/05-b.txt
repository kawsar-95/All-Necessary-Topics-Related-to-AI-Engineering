=== Synthetic data generation for evals
@sub When real examples are scarce — a new feature, rare edge cases, adversarial inputs — a model can generate test cases. Synthetic data scales coverage, but only human review and contamination checks keep it honest.
@icon 🛩️

## Generate from your own material
Self-Instruct showed that a model can bootstrap large instruction datasets from a small seed set, with filtering to remove low-quality and near-duplicate items [@selfinstruct]. For evals, the most useful variant is grounded generation: give the generator your documents, policies or API specs, and ask for questions whose answers can be verified in that material.

```python
GEN = """From the policy excerpt below, write {n} realistic customer questions.
Vary persona (new vs long-time customer), tone (calm vs angry),
language (English, Bangla, mixed) and difficulty (direct lookup vs multi-step).
For each, give the correct answer and the exact sentence that supports it.
Return JSON lines: {{"question", "answer", "evidence", "persona", "difficulty"}}.

Policy excerpt:
{chunk}"""
```

## Make it diverse on purpose
Left alone, generators produce bland, repetitive, easy cases. Steer diversity explicitly along axes — persona, intent, difficulty, language, length, adversarial style — and deduplicate by embedding similarity before keeping anything.

## Keep people in the loop
Synthetic cases need review: generated "correct answers" are sometimes wrong, and generated questions drift away from what real users ask. A practical rule is to review everything that will enter the release-gating set and sample-review the rest.

## Watch for contamination
If test items — or close paraphrases — appeared in a model's training data, scores measure memorisation rather than capability. Golchin and Surdeanu showed that contamination can be detected by prompting a model to complete partial test instances and comparing its completions with the originals [@contamination]. Practical defences: build eval sets from private, recent material; keep a held-out human-written set that is never published; and be sceptical when a new model scores suspiciously well on an old public benchmark.

| Use synthetic data for | Don't rely on it for |
|---|---|
| coverage of rare intents and edge cases | the final verdict on real-user quality |
| adversarial and red-team inputs | calibrating your LLM judge |
| multilingual variants of known cases | replacing production samples |
| load and latency tests | measuring user satisfaction |

@layman
**When you don't have enough real examples to test with, you can ask an AI to write practice questions** — based on your own documents, so the right answers can be checked.

**The trick is variety:** questions from new and old customers, calm and angry, in English and Bangla, easy and tricky. Otherwise the AI writes a hundred versions of the same easy question.

**People still review them,** because generated "correct answers" are sometimes wrong — and you keep some questions completely private, so no model could have seen them in advance.

>> **The flight simulator analogy:** pilots practise engine failures and storms in a simulator because waiting for real emergencies would be absurd. The scenarios are invented, but instructors design them carefully — and nobody earns a licence on simulator hours alone.

@bangla
বাস্তব উদাহরণ কম হলে — নতুন feature, বিরল edge case বা আক্রমণাত্মক input — মডেল দিয়ে test case তৈরি করা যায়। Self-Instruct দেখিয়েছে অল্প seed থেকে মডেল বড় dataset তৈরি করতে পারে, তবে ফিল্টার করে নিম্নমানের ও প্রায়-অভিন্ন অংশ বাদ দিতে হয় [@selfinstruct]।
- নিজের ডকুমেন্ট বা policy থেকে এমন প্রশ্ন তৈরি করান, যার উত্তর সেখানে যাচাই করা যায়।
- বৈচিত্র্য ইচ্ছাকৃতভাবে আনুন: persona, মেজাজ, ভাষা (English, বাংলা, মিশ্র), কাঠিন্য।
- Embedding-এর মিল দিয়ে প্রায়-অভিন্ন প্রশ্ন বাদ দিন।
- Release-gating set-এ ঢোকার আগে মানুষ দিয়ে সবকিছু যাচাই করুন।
- **Contamination**: test-এর প্রশ্ন মডেলের training data-তে থাকলে স্কোর আসলে মুখস্থবিদ্যা মাপে [@contamination]; তাই একটি ব্যক্তিগত, কখনো প্রকাশ না করা human-written set রাখুন।

>> **ফ্লাইট সিমুলেটরের উপমা:** পাইলটরা ইঞ্জিন বিকল বা ঝড়ের অনুশীলন সিমুলেটরে করেন, কারণ বাস্তব বিপদের অপেক্ষা করা অযৌক্তিক। পরিস্থিতিগুলো বানানো, কিন্তু প্রশিক্ষকেরা যত্ন করে নকশা করেন — আর শুধু সিমুলেটরের ঘণ্টা দিয়ে কেউ লাইসেন্স পান না।

=== Frameworks: Promptfoo, DeepEval, Inspect, Braintrust, OpenAI Evals
@sub Eval frameworks give you dataset handling, model runners, assertion libraries, judge templates, reports and CI integration. The concepts transfer between them; pick by language, workflow and where your results need to live.
@icon 📏

## The landscape
| Framework | Style | Good fit |
|---|---|---|
| Promptfoo | declarative YAML plus a CLI; side-by-side prompt and model comparisons [@promptfoo] | prompt iteration, CI gates, red-team scans |
| DeepEval | pytest-style test cases with a library of metrics, including G-Eval-style judges [@deepeval] | Python teams who want evals to feel like unit tests |
| Inspect | Python framework from the UK AI Security Institute, built around tasks, solvers and scorers [@inspect] | rigorous, reproducible evals, including agentic and safety evals |
| Braintrust | hosted platform linking datasets, experiments and production logs [@braintrust] | teams that want eval history and logs in one interface |
| OpenAI Evals | open-source framework and registry of evals [@oai-evals] | reusing community eval templates |

## A Promptfoo configuration
```yaml
# promptfooconfig.yaml — run with:  npx promptfoo eval
prompts:
  - file://prompts/support_v13.txt
  - file://prompts/support_v14.txt
providers:
  - id: openai:chat:<model-a>
  - id: anthropic:messages:<model-b>
tests:
  - vars:
      question: "My parcel A-1061 is late. Can I get a refund?"
    assert:
      - type: contains
        value: "A-1061"
      - type: llm-rubric
        value: "Explains the delay policy accurately and offers the correct next step without promising a refund"
      - type: latency
        threshold: 4000
```

## Choosing
- **Where do evals run?** In CI on every pull request (CLI-friendly tools), or as scheduled experiments (hosted platforms)?
- **Who writes them?** Engineers (code-first) or product and domain experts (YAML or a UI)?
- **Agent support.** Multi-step, tool-using tasks need trajectory access and sandboxed tools; check this before committing.
- **Data residency.** Hosted platforms store your prompts and outputs; self-hosted tools keep them in your network.

> good **The framework matters less than the dataset.** Teams switch frameworks; a well-curated golden set with clear expectations moves with them.

@layman
**Evaluation frameworks are toolkits that run your AI against your test questions and produce a report:** which version passed, which failed, where, and by how much.

**They differ mainly in style:** some are simple settings files run from the command line, some feel like a programmer's normal tests, and some are full websites with history and charts.

**Whichever you pick, the precious part is your collection of good test questions** — that's what you'd carry with you if you ever switched tools.

>> **The surveyor's toolkit analogy:** a builder can measure a room with a tape, a laser or a full surveying kit. The tools differ in speed and convenience, but the real asset is the plan — what needs measuring and what counts as within tolerance.

@bangla
Eval framework dataset পরিচালনা, মডেল চালানো, assertion, judge template, রিপোর্ট ও CI-সংযোগ দেয়।
- **Promptfoo**: YAML config ও CLI; prompt ও মডেল পাশাপাশি তুলনা [@promptfoo]।
- **DeepEval**: pytest-এর মতো test case, নানা মেট্রিক [@deepeval]।
- **Inspect**: UK AI Security Institute-এর Python framework — task, solver ও scorer [@inspect]।
- **Braintrust**: dataset, experiment ও production log এক জায়গায় [@braintrust]।
- **OpenAI Evals**: open-source framework ও eval registry [@oai-evals]।

কোনটি নেবেন: eval কোথায় চলবে (CI নাকি নির্ধারিত experiment), কে লিখবেন (engineer নাকি domain expert), agent-এর জন্য trajectory সমর্থন আছে কি না, আর ডেটা কোথায় থাকবে। মনে রাখবেন, framework-এর চেয়ে ভালো dataset অনেক বেশি মূল্যবান।

>> **জরিপকারীর যন্ত্রপাতির উপমা:** একজন নির্মাতা ফিতা, লেজার বা পূর্ণ জরিপ-যন্ত্র দিয়ে ঘর মাপতে পারেন। যন্ত্র গতি ও সুবিধায় আলাদা, কিন্তু আসল সম্পদ হলো নকশা — কী মাপতে হবে আর কতটুকু বিচ্যুতি গ্রহণযোগ্য।

=== Online evals on production traffic
@sub Offline sets go stale; real users keep finding new ways to break things. Online evals score a sample of live traffic continuously, so you see quality the way users experience it.
@icon 🏭

## What to run on live traffic
| Layer | Examples | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| Deterministic checks | JSON-schema validity, required fields, banned phrases, PII leakage, length limits | every response |
| Cheap model checks | a small classifier for refusals, language, topic or toxicity | every response, or most |
| LLM-judge rubrics | helpfulness, policy adherence, faithfulness to the retrieved context | a sample, for example 1–5% |
| Human review | ambiguous or high-risk cases, judge disagreements | a small, targeted queue |

## Run it off the hot path
Score asynchronously from the trace store (chapter 04) so evaluation never adds latency or new failure modes to user requests. Attach scores back to traces so you can slice them by prompt version, model, tenant and feature.

## Use online scores well
- **Trend, don't panic.** Online judge scores are noisy; alert on sustained shifts, not on a single bad answer.
- **Sample smartly.** Over-sample new features, high-value tenants and risky intents rather than sampling uniformly.
- **Feed the offline set.** Low-scoring production cases are the best candidates for the golden dataset.
- **Mind privacy.** Sending user conversations to a judge model is a data flow; it needs the same consent, redaction and retention rules as the original.

Practitioners writing up a year of building LLM products make similar recommendations: assertion-style checks built from real samples, model-based evaluation where needed but not as a silver bullet, and people reading samples of real inputs and outputs regularly [@oreilly-year].

@layman
**Tests written in advance can't predict everything real customers will do.** So you also grade a slice of real conversations as they happen.

**Simple automatic checks run on every answer** — is it in the right format, does it leak private details, is it absurdly long? A smarter AI grader looks at a small random sample in more depth, and people review the confusing cases.

**All of this happens in the background,** so customers never wait for the grading — and the worst real examples get added to your test collection.

>> **The bottling line analogy:** a bottling plant doesn't only test its recipe in the lab. Sensors check every bottle's fill level as it passes, and an inspector pulls random bottles off the line for a taste test. Bad batches get traced back, and the lab tests get updated.

@bangla
আগে লেখা test আসল ব্যবহারকারীর সব আচরণ আন্দাজ করতে পারে না। Online eval চলমান traffic-এর একটি নমুনা ধারাবাহিকভাবে মূল্যায়ন করে।
- প্রতিটি উত্তরে deterministic চেক: JSON-এর বৈধতা, আবশ্যিক field, নিষিদ্ধ শব্দ, PII ফাঁস, দৈর্ঘ্য।
- সস্তা classifier: প্রত্যাখ্যান, ভাষা, বিষয়, বিষাক্ততা।
- LLM-judge rubric: নমুনার ওপর (যেমন ১–৫%)।
- মানুষের review: অস্পষ্ট বা ঝুঁকিপূর্ণ case।

মূল্যায়ন মূল request-এর পথ থেকে আলাদা করে (asynchronously) চালান, স্কোর trace-এর সাথে যুক্ত করুন, একক খারাপ উত্তরে নয় বরং স্থায়ী পরিবর্তনে alert দিন, আর কম স্কোরের বাস্তব case golden dataset-এ যোগ করুন [@oreilly-year]। Judge মডেলে কথোপকথন পাঠানোও একটি ডেটা-প্রবাহ — consent, redaction ও retention নিয়ম মানতে হবে।

>> **বোতলজাতকরণ লাইনের উপমা:** বোতলজাতকরণ কারখানা শুধু ল্যাবে রেসিপি পরীক্ষা করে না। সেন্সর প্রতিটি বোতলের পরিমাণ মাপে, আর পরিদর্শক লাইন থেকে এলোমেলো বোতল তুলে স্বাদ পরীক্ষা করেন। খারাপ ব্যাচের উৎস খুঁজে ল্যাবের পরীক্ষাও হালনাগাদ করা হয়।

=== Red teaming and adversarial testing
@sub Red teaming means deliberately trying to make your system misbehave — leak data, follow injected instructions, produce harmful content, take unauthorised actions — before attackers or unlucky users do it for you.
@icon 🔐

## Manual and automated red teaming
Anthropic's study of red teaming released a dataset of 38,961 red-team attacks and examined how vulnerability changes across model sizes and training methods [@redteam-harms]. Perez et al. showed that one language model can generate test cases that find failures in another at scale [@redteam-lm]. In practice you combine both: people find creative, high-impact failures; automation turns them into large, repeatable suites.

## Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| garak | NVIDIA's open-source LLM vulnerability scanner, probing for prompt injection, jailbreaks, data leakage and more [@garak] |
| PyRIT | Microsoft's Python Risk Identification Tool for automating red teaming of generative AI systems [@pyrit] |
| Promptfoo red teaming | generates adversarial test cases against your own application configuration [@promptfoo] |
| AgentDojo | a benchmark environment for prompt-injection attacks and defences in tool-using agents [@agentdojo] |

## A practical process
1. **Threat-model the system.** What can it read, which tools can it call, who are its users? The OWASP Top 10 for LLM applications is a good checklist of risk categories [@owasp].
2. **Build an attack library** per category: direct and indirect injection, jailbreaks, PII extraction, harmful content, excessive agency, runaway cost.
3. **Automate** the library as an eval suite and track the attack success rate per category over time.
4. **Fix and re-test.** Every successful attack becomes a regression test (see the first section of this chapter).
5. **Repeat** on every major change — new tools, new model, new data sources — and on a schedule.

> danger **Agents raise the stakes.** For a chatbot, a successful attack produces bad text. For an agent with tools, it produces actions: refunds, emails, deleted records. Red-team tool-using agents with their tools connected to realistic sandboxes, not mocks.

@layman
**Red teaming is hiring people — and programs — to try to break your AI on purpose.** Can they trick it into revealing private information, following hidden instructions in a web page, saying something harmful, or doing something it shouldn't, like issuing a refund it wasn't allowed to give?

**Every trick that works becomes a permanent test,** and the tests run again whenever anything important changes.

**It matters most when the AI can take actions.** A chatbot that's tricked says something silly; an assistant with access to your systems that's tricked can do real damage.

>> **The locksmith analogy:** a bank hires professional locksmiths to try to pick its vault before burglars do. Each lock they open leads to a better lock — and the bank keeps hiring them, because burglars keep learning new tricks.

@bangla
**Red teaming** মানে ইচ্ছাকৃতভাবে সিস্টেমকে ভুল করানোর চেষ্টা — ডেটা ফাঁস, লুকানো নির্দেশ মানা, ক্ষতিকর বিষয়বস্তু, অননুমোদিত কাজ — আক্রমণকারী করার আগেই।
- Anthropic-এর গবেষণা ৩৮,৯৬১টি red-team আক্রমণের dataset প্রকাশ করেছে [@redteam-harms]; Perez et al. দেখিয়েছেন একটি মডেল আরেকটির ব্যর্থতা খুঁজতে বড় আকারে test তৈরি করতে পারে [@redteam-lm]।
- টুল: garak [@garak], PyRIT [@pyrit], Promptfoo red teaming, আর agent-এর prompt injection-এর জন্য AgentDojo [@agentdojo]।
- প্রক্রিয়া: threat model (OWASP Top 10 একটি ভালো চেকলিস্ট [@owasp]) → বিভাগভিত্তিক আক্রমণের সংগ্রহ → স্বয়ংক্রিয় eval suite → সংশোধন ও পুনঃপরীক্ষা → প্রতিটি বড় পরিবর্তনে আবার।

টুল-ব্যবহারকারী agent-এ ঝুঁকি বেশি: সফল আক্রমণ মানে শুধু খারাপ লেখা নয়, বাস্তব কাজ — refund, ইমেল, রেকর্ড মুছে ফেলা।

>> **তালাওয়ালার উপমা:** ব্যাংক পেশাদার তালাওয়ালা ভাড়া করে, যাতে চোরের আগেই তাঁরা ভল্ট খোলার চেষ্টা করেন। প্রতিটি খোলা তালা আরও ভালো তালার পথ দেখায় — আর ব্যাংক তাঁদের ডাকতেই থাকে, কারণ চোরেরাও নতুন কৌশল শেখে।
